Detailed Analysis Methodology

My analytical workflow at KPPN Surabaya I began with extracting and cleaning raw data from the SPAN (State Treasury and Budget System). Each transaction was validated to ensure account accuracy, budget availability, and the consistency of revenue and expenditure reports submitted by partner banks. This process established a reliable foundation for subsequent analysis and reporting.

To improve the efficiency of the daily cash reconciliation process, I utilized advanced Microsoft Excel features such as **VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP**, **INDEX-MATCH**, **Power Query**, and dynamic **Pivot Tables** for data extraction, transformation, and validation. The processed data was then connected to **Power BI** to build interactive dashboards that transformed raw financial data into clear visual insights, supporting monitoring, reporting, and data-driven decision-making.
