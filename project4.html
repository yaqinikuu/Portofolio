Simulation Free Equilibrium

When the Basic Reproduction Number exceeds 1 (R0 > 1), the model predicts that infected cases in both human and rodent populations will rise and stabilize at a persistent level, leading to a long-term endemic outbreak.

Simulation Endemic

When the Basic Reproduction Number is successfully kept below 1 (R0 < 1), the simulation predicts a steady decline in infected individuals toward zero, indicating the successful suppression of the outbreak.

Penjelasan Detail Metodologi Analisis

In this project, I developed a mathematical prediction model to simulate the transmission of disease from rats to humans and forecast its future impact. The simulation allows us to predict two primary scenarios: if the transmission rate is low (R0 = 0.62$), the outbreak is expected to subside and vanish completely within approximately 40–60 days. Conversely, if the transmission rate is high (R0 = 2.78), the model predicts a steady rise in cases, with the disease persisting as a long-term health issue (becoming endemic). Furthermore, the simulation predicts that a decline in public awareness regarding hygiene would lead to a sharp spike in human exposure and illness—mathematically demonstrating that adopting hygienic practices is the key to preventing a health crisis.
