European Sales Dashboard

Featured Project Documentation

The following screenshots showcase the European Sales Dashboard, an interactive data visualization project developed to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer distribution, and regional business trends. This dashboard presents key business insights through dynamic visualizations, including KPI monitoring, monthly sales trend analysis, product performance comparison, and geographical sales distribution across Europe. The interactive features allow users to explore patterns, identify high-performing products, and analyze regional performance to support data-driven decision-making.

1. Main Dashboard & Performance Overview

The main dashboard provides an overview of business performance through key metrics, including Total Sales ($1,042,204), Total Profit ($128,944), and Customer Count (706). It visualizes monthly sales trends, product performance rankings, and country-level sales distribution to identify overall business performance.

2. Interactive Product Analysis & Drill-Down View

The interactive drill-down feature enables deeper exploration of product performance by highlighting sales contributions from each category. Users can analyze top-performing products and compare them against the average benchmark through dynamic filtering and visual interactions.

3. Sales Trend Analysis & Geographic Insights

This visualization highlights monthly sales fluctuations throughout 2022 and identifies peak sales periods. The geographical map provides insights into regional sales distribution across European countries, helping users understand market performance and sales concentration.

Detailed Analysis Methodology

In analyzing business performance for the European Sales Dashboard project, the data processing workflow began with the extraction & cleaning of raw 2022 sales transaction records. Each order was thoroughly verified to ensure accurate product categorization, customer data validity, profit margin precision, and correct European regional mapping (such as Spain, France, and Germany).

The application of Advanced Excel functions—such as VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP, INDEX-MATCH, Power Query, and dynamic pivot tables—significantly accelerated the aggregation of monthly sales trends, calculation of product benchmark averages (60K threshold), and customer metrics. The processed data was then integrated into an interactive dashboard (Tableau/Power BI) to deliver actionable visual data insights—including monthly trend lines, product drill-down modals, and regional heatmaps—to support strategic managerial decision-making.
